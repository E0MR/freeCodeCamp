How to Make Egg Omelet (Ejja) ?

Ingredients

  • Eggs: 4 large eggs
  • Onion: 1 medium (finely chopped)
  • Oil or Butter: 2 tablespoons (for cooking)
  • Spices: Salt and black pepper (to taste), a pinch of cumin or paprika (optional)

Instructions

  1. Sauté the Onion: Heat one tablespoon of oil or butter in a skillet over medium heat, add the finely chopped onion, and sauté until soft and golden.
  2. Beat the Eggs: In a bowl, thoroughly whisk the 4 large eggs together with salt, black pepper, and a pinch of cumin or paprika.
  3. Combine Ingredients: Mix the sautéed onion (let it cool slightly first) into the beaten egg mixture.
  4. Cook and Serve: Heat the remaining oil or butter in the skillet, pour in the mixture, and cook over medium-low heat until set and golden brown.
